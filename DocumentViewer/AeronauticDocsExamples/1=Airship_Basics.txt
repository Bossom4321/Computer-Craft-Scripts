So, you want to build an airship do ya? Well allow me to explain some of the basics to you.

First and foremost, you are going to want to plan out an area to build the thing, a dry dock if you will. As airships once assembled are very heavy and cannot be moved by hand.

Some people perfer to give physics to the ship when done, and some prefer to create a single block and the just build on that physic block.

Either way to get a physics ship, you need a physics assembler. If you are assembling a whole structure, you can use either honey glue, or super glue. Honey glue is more recommened as it does not stick to any unwanted items as often.
