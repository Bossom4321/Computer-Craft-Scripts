Analog Vs Digital signal is an interesting one, as this is useful real life knowlegde. In real life this applies to computers a lot as electrical signals have watts and amps (Analog) and computers are binary/(on/off)/(true/false). The way computers can take analog signals is usually am I reciving a signal of some strength, if yes then on/1 if no then off/0, 

In minecraft sense when something is digital, it usually am I reciving redstone at all, or none at all. You can see this with a repeater as it will give a full strength no matter the input signal strength.

In minecraft when something is analog, it uses the redstones signal strength of 0-15, fun fact a lot of minecraft uses real computer aspects, such as using a 16 scale for a lot of things, like 0-15 is 16 different outputs, or 16 different color wools.

So if something gets stronger with more or less redstone signal it is analog, if an object is just on or off based on redstone it is digital.
