This is relatively easy, but do keep in mind that propellers tend to weigh a lot, so if it is on the back of your ship, you may wish to attach a tungsten cube of sorts to the front to balance it out.

You will want to use either a andesite propeller, or a propeller bearing.

A andesite propeller is better for smaller ships, as it is easier to build and more compact.

A propeller bearing works similarly to a windmill, in the sense that you have to build a structure of the propeller using sails or symetric sails

Then you just have to give them rotational power forward or backwards depending on which way you want to go, and you are on your way.
