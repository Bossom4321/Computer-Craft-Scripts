To successfully turn your airship the recommened method is by using sails as a rudder of sorts. The preferred way is to use a swivel bearing, a swivel bearing to turn requires using a gear on the side with the gear. The shaft on the bottom is for giving rotation to objects on the bearing itself, for instance for slightly faster turning you can install your propeller on your rudder.

You will likely need superglue to assemble your bearing as it works similarly to how create contraptions work, unlike create contraptions however, once assembled, you can still place blocks on them like the airships. Think of it like a mini airship on your airship.

You can then turn the bearing left or right depending on the way you want to go, and as long as you are moving forward or backwards, the sail will the push the back of the ship in a similar way to how airplanes work.
